# CYA — Phase 11.5 Partner & Expert Linking Kit v0.1

**Status:** READY FOR USE AFTER +14 DAY MIGRATION SAFETY REVIEW  
**Purpose:** make legitimate references to Corporate Yoga Australia easy, accurate and canonical.

## Principle

This is not an SEO link-exchange kit.

Use it only where a client, expert, venue, event partner, association, publisher or collaborator already has a genuine reason to mention CYA.

Do not ask for:

- exact-match keyword anchors;
- reciprocal-link quotas;
- paid dofollow placement;
- unrelated directory listings;
- claims the partner cannot substantiate;
- links solely to manipulate rankings.

A natural brand or descriptive link is preferred.

## Approved short descriptions

Partners should use their own natural editorial wording where possible. These are safe starting points, not mandatory scripts.

### One line

Corporate Yoga Australia provides human-led workplace Yoga, Pilates, mindfulness, wellbeing workshops and tailored wellbeing programs for Australian organisations.

### Short description

Corporate Yoga Australia provides human-led workplace wellbeing experiences for Australian organisations, including Yoga, Pilates, mindfulness, workshops and tailored programs delivered onsite and online.

### Program-focused description

Corporate Yoga Australia helps organisations plan and deliver practical workplace wellbeing programs combining human-led experiences, coordination, flexible delivery and ongoing support.

### Events-focused description

Corporate Yoga Australia provides workplace and event wellbeing experiences including Yoga, Pilates, mindfulness, movement resets and expert-led sessions for teams, conferences and workplace settings.

Do not add medical, productivity, ROI or guaranteed-outcome claims unless independently approved and evidenced.

## Natural link wording examples

Good natural examples include:

- Corporate Yoga Australia
- workplace Yoga with Corporate Yoga Australia
- Corporate Yoga Australia's workplace wellbeing programs
- workplace Pilates sessions from Corporate Yoga Australia
- CYA's workplace mindfulness and meditation services

The publisher should choose wording that reads naturally in its own page. Do not request repetitive keyword-rich anchors across partners.

## Partner reference pack

When a genuine partner asks for information, provide only what is useful:

1. the most relevant canonical URL;
2. one approved short description above;
3. approved logo/image assets where appropriate;
4. correct organisation/founder naming;
5. event/session specifics that have been verified for that relationship;
6. contact details only where the partner genuinely needs them.

Keep the request simple. Do not send an SEO rationale.

## Expert Experience reference pattern

For a genuine CYA expert/session, the ideal authority loop is:

1. CYA publishes a substantive canonical expert/session page;
2. the page includes the expert's verified bio, professional context and relevant resources;
3. CYA links to the expert's credible professional site/profile where useful;
4. the expert can naturally reference the CYA session/page from their own site, newsletter or social/profile context if they choose;
5. related CYA Insights and commercial service pages are connected through sensible internal links.

This is editorial attribution, not reciprocal-link trading.

## Minimum expert-page evidence pack

Before publishing an expert profile/session page, obtain or verify:

- preferred professional name;
- current role/title;
- relevant qualification/credential claims intended for publication;
- organisation/website/profile URL;
- short approved biography;
- approved image/headshot;
- topic/session title and scope;
- resources/references the expert is comfortable associating with the session;
- permission to publish the supplied information.

Do not infer credentials from social profiles or old bios.

## Client / workplace references

Where a client legitimately wants to reference CYA, the reference may point to:

- Home for a broad supplier/partner mention;
- a specific service page if the relationship genuinely involved that service;
- an approved Case Study only after the separate client evidence/permission gate is satisfied.

Do not ask a client to publish a case study, testimonial, outcome or metric purely to gain a backlink.

## Event / venue references

Useful legitimate placements can include:

- speaker/facilitator listings;
- event wellbeing activity pages;
- venue partner/supplier resources;
- conference itinerary pages;
- retreat or delegate wellbeing guides.

Prefer the most relevant service/resource destination rather than sending every event link to Home.

## Updating existing links after migration

Only begin proactive direct-link reclamation after the +14-day migration safety review is satisfactory.

Priority order:

1. high-trust editorial links pointing through obsolete URLs;
2. high-trust links using non-canonical host variants;
3. warm partner links that can be updated easily as part of normal relationship maintenance.

Do not chase every historical redirect. A healthy one-hop 301 is acceptable.

## Tracking

For each proactive update or earned reference, record:

- source organisation/domain;
- source page;
- destination CYA page;
- relationship lane;
- date requested / date live;
- whether the link is editorially natural;
- referral traffic or useful enquiries when available;
- any future collaboration value.

Do not make raw link count the success metric.
