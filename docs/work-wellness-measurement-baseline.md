## Success signals

### Early signal
- authority page indexed and earning impressions;
- CYA appears for exact and near-exact Work Wellness queries;
- authority page earns impressions for broader workplace wellbeing terms;
- internal journeys continue from the authority page to service or contact pages.

### Stronger signal
- independent sources mention Corporate Yoga Australia alongside Work Wellness;
- AI answers cite CYA when defining or discussing Work Wellness/workday wellbeing;
- branded or navigational searches containing CYA + Work Wellness emerge;
- buyer language begins repeating the concept unprompted.

### Level C escalation evidence
Do not escalate to deliberate category-building from rankings alone. Require evidence from at least three areas: sustained search demand, third-party language adoption/citation, AI association, buyer adoption, or attributable commercial journeys.
