# Work Wellness Territory 02 — Review Gate

Review the implementation as one semantic system rather than judging isolated uses of the phrase.

## Review pages

1. Homepage — establishes the brand expression and routes to the authority definition.
2. What is Work Wellness? — canonical definition, category distinction and guardrail.
3. About CYA — explains why the concept belongs to the CYA story and proposition.
4. Online Wellbeing — demonstrates Work Wellness through continuity and Wellbeing Studio.
5. Workplace Wellbeing Programs — remains the established commercial/category anchor and should not be renamed around Work Wellness.
6. Insights — authority/discovery home without adding a Work Wellness navigation category.

## Pass criteria

- A reader can understand Work Wellness without being told it is a new industry category.
- Workplace wellbeing remains visibly broader than CYA's Work Wellness contribution.
- The concept expands CYA beyond a yoga-only perception without obscuring workplace yoga search equity.
- Wellbeing Studio feels like practical continuity within CYA, not a separate software brand.
- Work Wellness appears selectively and contextually rather than as repeated exact-match copy.
- Psychosocial-risk boundaries remain explicit and credible.
- The site's machine-readable data associates CYA with both established workplace wellbeing topics and the Work Wellness concept.

## Non-goals

- no main navigation item;
- no service rename;
- no separate domain or microsite;
- no proprietary-category claim;
- no Level C category-building decision during this review gate.
