# CYA — Phase 11.5 Competitor Authority Gap Shortlist

**Prepared:** 26 August 2026  
**Status:** INVESTIGATION QUEUE / NO OUTREACH BEFORE +14 DAY MIGRATION SAFETY REVIEW

## Purpose

Identify a small number of credible authority ecosystems that currently reference comparable Australian workplace-wellbeing providers and may therefore represent legitimate future editorial/relationship opportunities for Corporate Yoga Australia.

This is **pattern evidence**, not a mechanical backlink prospect list.

Every candidate must pass the same test before outreach:

> Is there a real editorial, professional or business reason for this organisation to reference CYA if Google did not exist?

## Strongest opportunity patterns

### 1. Australian business / workplace editorial

#### The Times Australia

**Domain:** `thetimes.com.au`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** contextual editorial coverage of an Australian workplace-wellbeing provider in a technology/workplace story.  
**Why it matters:** demonstrates that workplace-wellbeing providers can earn genuine Australian business/editorial citations around substantive stories rather than generic service pages.

**CYA eligibility:** only when there is a real story, such as:

- useful Australian workplace-wellbeing planning insight;
- a credible founder/business milestone;
- a substantive expert initiative;
- a genuine event/campaign finding;
- a useful annual planning/resource asset.

**Do not:** pitch a press release purely for a backlink.

#### Business Acumen

**Domain:** `businessacumen.biz`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** business/technology editorial story linking to a workplace-wellbeing provider.  
**Why it matters:** reinforces the value of useful business-facing stories rather than SEO guest posts.

**CYA eligibility:** similar to above; only where CYA can contribute a credible business/workplace angle.

### 2. Commercial property / workplace ecosystem

#### Commercial Property Guide

**Domain:** `commercialpropertyguide.com.au`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** contextual business/workplace article citing the founder of a workplace-wellbeing provider.  
**Why it matters:** commercial property is strongly adjacent to CYA's real workplace delivery model and existing business-events authority.

**Potential CYA contribution themes:**

- practical wellbeing activation in office/property environments;
- tenant/community wellbeing programming;
- workplace/event movement formats;
- planning physical space for accessible Yoga/Pilates/mindfulness sessions.

**Eligibility:** only where CYA has a genuine property/workplace relationship or useful editorial contribution.

#### workit

**Domain:** `workit.com.au`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** remote-team/workplace article linking contextually to a workplace-wellbeing provider.  
**Why it matters:** useful fit with CYA's remote/hybrid delivery and online wellbeing continuity.

**Potential CYA contribution themes:**

- practical remote-team wellbeing resets;
- short live movement/mindfulness formats;
- hybrid participation guidance;
- avoiding the "resource library only" approach to distributed wellbeing.

### 3. HR / employment ecosystem

#### Employment Innovations

**Domain:** `employmentinnovations.com`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** employee rewards/recognition article citing workplace-wellbeing content.  
**Why it matters:** HR/People audiences are commercially relevant to CYA's program and workshop offer.

**Potential CYA contribution themes:**

- introducing wellbeing activities without overcomplicating procurement;
- participation/accessibility considerations;
- practical program planning;
- useful employee wellbeing calendar/planning resources.

**Eligibility:** editorial usefulness first; do not pitch service promotion disguised as HR advice.

### 4. Professional / association ecosystem

#### EAPAA

**Domain:** `eapaa.org.au`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** provider profile/listing for Workplace Wellness Australia.  
**Why it matters:** professional associations can carry strong trust signals.

**Important boundary:** CYA is not to be represented as an EAP or psychological service provider unless that is genuinely part of an approved product relationship and evidence set.

**CYA eligibility:** only if a legitimate expert, professional or partnership context develops. Do not pursue a generic association listing simply for authority.

#### Consult Clarity

**Domain:** `consultclarity.org`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** resource/list pages referencing corporate wellness workshop providers and professional-development providers.  
**Why it matters:** shows that substantive provider/resource roundups can create discovery and citation opportunities.

**CYA eligibility:** only after confirming editorial standards and a genuine fit. Avoid low-quality "best provider" lists where inclusion is effectively paid or arbitrary.

### 5. Workplace product / employer resource ecosystem

#### BFX Furniture

**Domain:** `bfx.com.au`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** long-form employee wellbeing article cites workplace-wellbeing content.  
**Why it matters:** office/workplace product companies often publish content for the same HR/facilities/workplace audience CYA serves.

**CYA opportunity:** useful source material rather than promotional insertion—for example, evidence-safe workplace movement or program planning guidance.

#### Astute Financial

**Domain:** `astutefinancial.com.au`  
**Observed competitor pattern:** health/stress editorial content references The Wellness Workshop.  
**Why it matters:** demonstrates cross-industry employers/business networks citing credible wellbeing resources.

**CYA eligibility:** only where an editorial topic genuinely overlaps CYA's expertise or an expert-session resource.

1. Commercial Property Guide — strongest property/workplace adjacency.
2. workit — remote/hybrid workplace adjacency.
3. Employment Innovations — HR/People audience fit.
4. Business Acumen — credible Australian business editorial potential.
5. The Times Australia — credible business/editorial potential when there is a real story.

### Tier B — conditional / relationship dependent

6. EAPAA — only with legitimate professional/EAP-adjacent context.
7. BFX Furniture — workplace-content ecosystem.
8. Astute Financial — broader business/wellbeing editorial ecosystem.
9. Consult Clarity — only after quality/editorial standards are verified.

## What this evidence does **not** justify

Do not infer that CYA should:

- join every association that links to a competitor;
- copy competitor content;
- create pages to target these organisations;
- cold-email publishers asking for a backlink;
- submit to generic "best wellness provider" lists;
- add EAP/psychological claims to match competitors;
- manufacture survey data or PR stories.

## First use after +14 day safety review

For each Tier A candidate:

1. inspect current editorial themes and standards;
2. identify whether CYA has a real relationship, expert or useful asset that fits;
3. map the most relevant CYA canonical page/resource;
4. choose **contribute / relationship / hold**;
5. only then draft outreach.

No outreach volume target is required.

---

**Decision principle:** competitor backlinks reveal ecosystems and editorial patterns. They do not create entitlement to a link.
