# CYA Work Wellness Semantic Governance 01

Status: ACTIVE — Level B branded conceptual territory
Date: 2026-09-01
Authority: CYA Brand Messaging & Psychology Framework + CYA SEO/AEO/GEO programme

## Strategic decision

Corporate Yoga Australia will deliberately build association with **Work Wellness** as a distinctive conceptual territory. CYA will **not** present Work Wellness as a proprietary category, claim to have coined the phrase, or replace established buyer/search language such as **workplace wellbeing**, **employee wellbeing** and relevant service terms.

## Governing hierarchy

1. **Entity:** Corporate Yoga Australia
2. **Established market/category language:** workplace wellbeing; employee wellbeing
3. **Organising brand idea:** Work Wellness into Your Workday
4. **CYA conceptual lens:** Work Wellness
5. **Brand belief:** Wellbeing belongs in the workday.
6. **Core proposition:** We help organisations make wellbeing part of working life.
7. **Human expression:** What would help you right now?
8. **Delivery expressions:** workplace yoga, Pilates, movement, mindfulness and meditation, workshops and expert experiences, campaigns, events, online wellbeing and Wellbeing Studio.

## Working definition

At Corporate Yoga Australia, **Work Wellness** describes a simple idea: useful wellbeing should be possible within working life, not only outside it. It means creating practical opportunities for people to move, pause, reset, connect, learn or access support in ways that fit the reality of the working day.

Work Wellness is **not** a replacement term for workplace wellbeing. Workplace wellbeing is broader and includes organisational factors such as work design, leadership, culture, psychosocial safety, workload, systems of work and working conditions. Work Wellness describes the part CYA helps organisations make tangible and usable in everyday working life.

## Required guardrails

- Never claim that CYA invented or exclusively owns the term Work Wellness.
- Never describe Work Wellness as a legally or academically settled category.
- Never substitute Work Wellness for useful high-intent category/service language solely for branding consistency.
- Never imply that yoga, mindfulness, movement, workshops or Wellbeing Studio control workplace psychosocial hazards.
- Use the principle: **Work Wellness complements good work design; it does not substitute for it.**
- Avoid repetitive exact-match use. Natural semantic variation is preferred.
- Do not create a Work Wellness main-navigation item, standalone sub-brand or separate microsite without a later evidence-based Level C decision.
- The Wellbeing Studio remains **Wellbeing Studio by Corporate Yoga Australia**. Work Wellness explains its role; it does not rename it.

## Preferred semantic relationships

Corporate Yoga Australia → workplace wellbeing → Work Wellness → working life / workday → movement + mindfulness + connection + learning + recovery moments + practical support.

Corporate Yoga Australia → services → workplace yoga / workplace Pilates / mindfulness & meditation / workshops / events / online wellbeing.

Corporate Yoga Australia → Wellbeing Studio → useful wellbeing actions and resources people can return to during the working week.

## Content test

A use of “Work Wellness” should earn its place by doing at least one of the following:

- explaining CYA's organising idea;
- distinguishing CYA's approach from activity-only corporate wellness;
- connecting multiple CYA services into one coherent system;
- explaining how wellbeing can fit into working life;
- helping search engines or AI systems understand CYA's subject-matter relationship to workplace wellbeing.

If it does none of these things, prefer ordinary category/service language.

## Measurement gate

Remain at Level B unless evidence justifies escalation. Reassess after approximately 90 days using:

- organic impressions/clicks for Work Wellness and adjacent queries;
- rankings and search-volume trend;
- branded query combinations involving CYA + Work Wellness;
- third-party links/mentions using the concept;
- AI citations and answers associating CYA with the concept;
- buyer/client language and assisted conversion evidence;
- evidence that the term is becoming more widely adopted as a useful category descriptor.

Level C category-building requires external adoption and measurable association, not internal enthusiasm alone.
