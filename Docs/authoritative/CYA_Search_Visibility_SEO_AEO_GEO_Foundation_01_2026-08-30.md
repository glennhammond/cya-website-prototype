# CYA Search Visibility - SEO, AEO & GEO Foundation 01

**Status:** Foundation complete / implementation qualified / no production release authorised  
**Prepared:** 30 August 2026  
**Implementation branch:** `feat/search-visibility-foundation-01`  
**Production baseline:** current Squarespace site  
**Replacement-site authority:** `glennhammond/cya-website-prototype`  

## 1. Executive decision

Corporate Yoga Australia should operate one search-visibility system across conventional search, AI-generated answers and future agent-assisted discovery.

SEO, AEO and GEO are not separate publishing programs. The shared foundation is:

1. technically retrievable canonical pages;
2. direct and useful answers to real buyer questions;
3. a consistent, unambiguous CYA entity;
4. first-party expertise and evidence worth citing;
5. genuine corroboration beyond CYA's own website; and
6. measurement that distinguishes observable evidence from inference.

The redesign already supplies most of the technical retrieval layer. Foundation 01 closes the remaining governance and implementation gaps by adding:

- a governed 36-prompt Australian buyer-question portfolio;
- a page-by-page answer-intent and evidence map;
- a stronger, connected CYA and founder entity graph;
- persistent attribution for identifiable AI-assisted referrals;
- a repeatable technical qualification gate; and
- a prioritised content, evidence, corroboration and measurement program.

No Squarespace change, redirect, merge, Vercel production deployment or DNS action is authorised by this phase.

## 2. Governing constraints

The following existing decisions remain binding:

- The live Squarespace site remains the production comparison baseline until a separately approved cutover.
- Home retains broad `corporate yoga` authority.
- Commercial pages own transactional intent; Insights supports rather than competes with them.
- Proven `/blog/...` URLs remain protected unless evidence supports a different migration decision.
- The public label may be **Insights** while migration-protected `/blog` addresses remain unchanged.
- One-off sessions are legitimate purchases. Programs must not be forced as the only valid engagement.
- Wellbeing Studio is presented as **Wellbeing Studio by Corporate Yoga Australia**, providing continuity around CYA's human-led service.
- CYA must not be positioned as an EAP, clinical provider or replacement for psychosocial risk management.
- Wellbeing activities must not be represented as substitutes for hazard identification, worker consultation, work design, controls, professional advice or statutory duties.
- Case studies remain noindex until permission, factual evidence and asset-rights gates pass.
- `glennhammond/cya-site` is deprecated historical evidence and must not be used as an implementation base.

## 3. Current-source position

### 3.1 Platform guidance

The technical and editorial approach follows current primary platform guidance.

- Google states that its generative Search features remain rooted in core Search indexing, ranking and quality systems. It advises valuable non-commodity content, a clear technical structure and people-first usefulness, while explicitly rejecting supposed requirements such as `llms.txt`, artificial micro-chunking, AI-only rewrites and inauthentic mentions.  
  Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- OpenAI states that public sites should not block `OAI-SearchBot` if they want to be eligible for inclusion in ChatGPT search summaries and links. It also states that ChatGPT search referrals include `utm_source=chatgpt.com`.  
  Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq
- Bing's AI Performance reporting exposes citation activity, cited pages and grounding-query phrases, and recommends clearer structure, useful depth and evidence.  
  Source: https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview
- Safe Work Australia confirms that PCBUs must eliminate psychosocial risks or minimise them so far as is reasonably practicable. This is the governing boundary for CYA's supportive, non-compliance positioning.  
  Source: https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/mental-health/psychosocial-hazards

### 3.2 Foundation principle

> Make CYA easy to retrieve, easy to understand, accurate to describe and genuinely worth citing.

This phase does not add `llms.txt`, mass-produce long-tail pages, create unsupported location doorway pages, publish generic AI-generated articles or seek manufactured mentions.

## 5. Governed buyer-question and prompt set

The canonical machine-readable portfolio is:

`config/ai-visibility-prompts.json`

It contains 36 prompts across:

1. entity identification;
2. service discovery;
3. program procurement;
4. engagement and authority;
5. campaigns and events; and
6. decision support.

Every prompt has:

- a stable ID;
- an exact Australian English prompt;
- an intent;
- a declared page owner;
- commercial value; and
- evidence risk.

### Monthly recording standard

For every available platform and prompt, record:

| Field | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Platform/access state | Record the exact experience used; do not combine ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode and Copilot into one result. |
| Date/location | Use Australian context and record the review date. |
| Mention | Yes/no plus the form of the mention. |
| Citation | Yes/no plus the exact cited CYA page. |
| Accuracy | Correct, partly correct or materially incorrect, with the specific issue. |
| Competitors | Record included providers and the reason implied by the response. |
| Influencing sources | Record third-party pages used to ground the answer. |
| Change | Compare with the prior month by prompt and theme. |
| Commercial implication | Prioritise action only where it affects buyer confidence or qualified discovery. |

Do not calculate a false universal AI rank. Useful roll-ups are:

- prompt coverage rate;
- citation rate;
- accurate-description rate;
- owned-page citation mix;
- competitor inclusion frequency;
- materially incorrect answer count; and
- AI-assisted enquiries.

## 8. Service and Insights answer templates

### 8.1 Commercial service/program template

Each main page should answer, in the order that best fits the buyer:

1. What is this service?
2. Who is it for?
3. What workplace situation does it fit?
4. What do employees experience?
5. Which formats and durations are real?
6. What space, equipment or preparation is needed?
7. Which delivery modes and locations are available?
8. Who leads or coordinates it, and what expertise is verified?
9. What evidence exists?
10. What is in and out of scope?
11. Is one session, a series or an ongoing program appropriate?
12. What is the next useful action?

The opening paragraph must directly answer the central question. It must not require a buyer or retrieval system to infer the offer from brand language.

### 8.2 Comparison module

Use a compact visible table only when it helps a real decision, for example:

- Yoga versus Pilates;
- mat versus chair/desk formats;
- meditation versus mindfulness versus breathwork;
- one-off event versus recurring program;
- onsite versus online versus hybrid; and
- live session versus Studio continuity.

Avoid creating a separate page for every comparison.

### 8.3 Insights template

Every substantive Insight requires:

1. a direct answer or useful summary near the start;
2. one primary reader intent;
3. one declared commercial page owner;
4. visible author and, where needed, expert reviewer;
5. original publication and review dates;
6. sources close to material claims;
7. first-party examples only where permission and context exist;
8. clear clinical, legal or compliance scope boundaries where relevant;
9. contextual links to the commercial owner; and
10. an update trigger rather than a decorative freshness date.

### 8.4 Editorial authority correction

Several current replacement-site Insights display Glenn Hammond or Corporate Yoga Australia as author. Before publication, practitioner, health, psychosocial and specialist content must identify the person who supplied or reviewed the substantive expertise. Glenn may be credited for editorial production where accurate, but editorial production must not be presented as practitioner authority.

This remains a content-approval action. Foundation 01 does not invent reviewer attribution.

## 9. Crawler and indexation controls

### Implemented and qualified

- Production `robots.txt` permits public crawlers through the wildcard rule.
- Vercel Preview environments disallow all crawlers.
- `OAI-SearchBot` is not blocked, so the production candidate remains eligible for ChatGPT search discovery.
- The canonical sitemap contains only index-qualified commercial and Insights URLs.
- Evidence-dependent and operational routes remain outside the sitemap and explicitly noindex where required.
- No unsupported `llms.txt` dependency has been introduced.
- Canonical pages render meaningful text in server-generated HTML.

### Training and search are separate choices

`OAI-SearchBot` relates to search discovery. `GPTBot` relates to model training controls. CYA can make a separate future policy decision about training without blocking ChatGPT search eligibility. Foundation 01 does not silently change that policy.

### IndexNow

IndexNow is a useful post-launch discovery accelerator for Bing and participating systems, but it is not a substitute for correct redirects, canonicals or sitemap maintenance. Implement it only when:

- the production domain is on the replacement site;
- a key is held securely;
- submission is limited to real created, updated or deleted canonical URLs; and
- retry/observability are defined.

It is not a Foundation 01 blocker.

## 12. Measurement configuration

### 12.1 Source hierarchy

1. **Google Search Console** - Google discovery, indexing and generative Search performance where available.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance** - citations, cited pages, grounding queries, topics and competitor comparison where available.
3. **GA4** - sessions and outcomes from identifiable AI referrals.
4. **HubSpot** - qualified enquiry and discovery context.
5. **Ahrefs** - competitive search, content and referring-domain intelligence; not a substitute for first-party conversion data.
6. **Governed prompt review** - qualitative cross-platform inclusion and accuracy patterns.

### 12.2 Implemented AI-assisted referral handling

The replacement site already captured UTMs and click IDs. Foundation 01 now also:

- preserves the initial referrer across a multi-page visit;
- recognises identifiable referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot;
- classifies them as `ai-search` without treating all Bing or Google traffic as AI;
- preserves ChatGPT's `utm_source=chatgpt.com`; and
- passes the discovery channel, source and original referrer into the governed HubSpot discovery context on enquiry.

This does not circumvent browser privacy or claim attribution where no distinguishable signal exists.

### 12.3 Required GA4/HubSpot reporting views after release

- AI-assisted sessions by source;
- landing page;
- engaged sessions;
- enquiry completion;
- qualified enquiry status;
- assisted conversions;
- branded search trend after AI exposure; and
- source/landing-page combinations that create commercially useful enquiries.

## 15. Success criteria

Foundation 01 succeeds when CYA becomes more consistently and accurately retrievable for commercially relevant Australian workplace questions, not when it merely accumulates more AI mentions.

Track:

- zero material entity inaccuracies across the governed branded prompts;
- increasing inclusion and citation for high-value non-branded prompts;
- a growing share of citations to the correct CYA commercial or authority owner;
- reduced competitor-only answers in Pilates, mindfulness, programs, workshops and engagement;
- increasing genuine third-party corroboration;
- AI-assisted visits that engage with relevant pages; and
- qualified enquiries influenced by AI discovery.

Numerical thresholds should be set only after the first true cross-platform baseline is captured. This avoids manufacturing a target from a public-search proxy.
