## Canonical migration tests

### Protect

Home must retain strong broad `corporate yoga` authority.

### Consolidate

After migration, monitor whether:

- `workplace yoga` begins to prefer `/workplace-yoga` where specialist intent warrants it;
- wellbeing-program queries begin to associate with `/workplace-wellbeing-programs`;
- Pilates terms begin to associate with `/workplace-pilates`;
- mindfulness/meditation terms consolidate on `/meditation-mindfulness`;
- workshop terms associate with `/workplace-wellbeing-workshops`;
- online/hybrid terms associate with `/online-wellbeing`.

Do not force a ranking transfer where Google continues to prefer Home for genuinely broad category language.

## Decision thresholds

### Protect

- Home remains top 3 for `corporate yoga` and retains healthy impressions/clicks.
- Protected Insights retain useful page-one or near-page-one visibility.

### Investigate

- Home loses material `corporate yoga` visibility for 7+ days;
- a legacy URL remains Google's preferred URL after the normal migration window;
- a new specialist page gets impressions but a clearly wrong page keeps ranking.

### Improve

After migration stabilises, prioritise terms where CYA sits approximately positions 4–15 or where new canonical pages accumulate impressions with weak CTR.

### Hold

Do not react to launch-day or first-week volatility unless a technical P0 condition exists.
