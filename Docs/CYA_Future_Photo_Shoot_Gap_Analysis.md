# CYA Future Photo Shoot — Gap Analysis

Assessed against the 90-image provisional library (`Docs/CYA_Prototype_v1_Image_Audit.md`) after the prototype image-curation pass (`Docs/CYA_Prototype_v1_Image_Mapping.md`). Each requirement below is rated **Covered**, **Partially covered** or **Gap**, based on what the library actually contains — not what would be nice to have.

---

### 1. One-off workplace session
- **Status:** Partially covered — `Workplace Wellness Lunch & Learn Session another Workspace Brisbane.jpg` and the "Wellbeing Workshops" desk-stretch series read well as a single session, but both are the same handful of shoots reused across several pages.
- **Business purpose:** Prove a one-off session is a complete, credible offer — the site's most repeated commercial claim.
- **Page placement:** Home hero, Workplace Wellbeing hero.
- **Recommended subject:** A single facilitator delivering one clearly bounded session (not a multi-week series) to a small real team.
- **Setting:** A genuine client office, meeting room or breakout space — not a studio.
- **Orientation:** Landscape primary, with a portrait crop of the same moment for mobile.
- **Crop requirements:** Wide enough to show the room and at least 4–6 participants; must survive a tight square crop for card use.
- **Participants:** 4–8 people, mixed age/gender/ethnicity.
- **Wardrobe:** Everyday office clothing, not athleisure.
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight where possible; avoid harsh overhead fluorescent glare.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Include at least one visibly seated/chair-based participant.
- **Permissions required:** Client site consent, individual participant consent/release, CYA commercial usage licence.

### 2. Desk-based movement
- **Status:** Partially covered — good concept coverage (`Corporate Yoga Australia - Services - Workplace Wellness.webp`, `1.00_00_04_28.Still001.jpg`) but both are low-resolution (a compressed web asset and a video still respectively).
- **Business purpose:** Directly answers "no mat, no studio, no special clothes required."
- **Page placement:** Movement hero, Workplace Wellbeing modality card.
- **Recommended subject:** Multiple people (not just one) stretching at their own desks, at least one male and one female subject, at least one wheelchair-user or seated-only variation.
- **Setting:** Real open-plan office with visible monitors/desks.
- **Orientation:** Landscape.
- **Crop requirements:** Keep full upper-body gesture and desk context; do not crop to a headless torso.
- **Participants:** 2–3 individuals captured separately, varied roles/attire.
- **Wardrobe:** Business and business-casual.
- **Lighting:** Office ambient + window light.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** At least one visibly older participant; at least one participant using a mobility aid or adapted seating if available and consented.
- **Permissions required:** Individual and site consent.

### 3. Seated movement
- **Status:** Partially covered — the chair-based meditation session (`Workplace Wellness Lunch & Learn Session another Workspace Brisbane.jpg/.webp`) is strong for mindfulness, but there is no image of seated *movement* (chair yoga/mobility) specifically, as distinct from seated stillness.
- **Business purpose:** Chair-based options are central to the inclusion claim ("no mat experience... assumed").
- **Page placement:** Movement page "useful formats."
- **Recommended subject:** A small group doing active chair-based stretches/mobility (arm circles, seated twists), not meditation.
- **Setting:** Office meeting room, chairs (not yoga mats).
- **Orientation:** Landscape.
- **Crop requirements:** Show hands and torso rotation clearly — this is the evidence of "movement," not just sitting.
- **Participants:** 3–5, mixed age, at least one visibly larger-bodied participant.
- **Wardrobe:** Office attire.
- **Lighting:** Bright, even.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Central to this gap — seated movement is itself an accessibility-forward format.
- **Permissions required:** Individual and site consent.

### 5. Mindfulness
- **Status:** Partially covered — good anonymous hand/mudra close-ups and one strong group relaxation photo, but nothing shows a facilitator actively leading a *seated, eyes-open* mindfulness moment at a desk or in a meeting room (as opposed to lying down or solo).
- **Business purpose:** Supports the "practical pause," not a yoga-studio meditation cliché.
- **Page placement:** Workplace Wellbeing mindfulness card.
- **Recommended subject:** A facilitator guiding 2–4 people through a brief seated breathing moment at a desk or table.
- **Setting:** Office desk/meeting table.
- **Orientation:** Landscape.
- **Crop requirements:** Faces visible, calm not clinical.
- **Participants:** 2–4.
- **Wardrobe:** Office attire.
- **Lighting:** Soft, warm.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Avoid closed-eyes-only imagery throughout — vary with alert, open-eyed calm.
- **Permissions required:** Individual and site consent.

### 6. Breathwork

- **Recommended subject:** A facilitator demonstrating a simple breathing cue (hand on chest, relaxed shoulders) with 1–2 participants following along.
- **Setting:** Office or neutral indoor space.
- **Orientation:** Landscape or square.

- **Participants:** 1 facilitator + 1–2 participants.
- **Wardrobe:** Office attire.
- **Lighting:** Soft, natural.

### 8. Conference activation
- **Status:** Gap — no image shows a large-format conference/event space (stage, auditorium, branded event signage minus a specific client name, large seated audience). Current Conference hero is a facilitator-led group in what reads as a community hall, not a conference floor.
- **Business purpose:** The page's primary audience (event organisers) needs to see genuine event-scale delivery to trust CYA at that scale.
- **Page placement:** Conferences and Events hero.
- **Recommended subject:** A CYA activation at an actual multi-day conference or large offsite — movement break with a large seated/standing audience, ideally with a stage or generic (non-client-branded) event signage visible.
- **Setting:** Conference centre, large hotel function room, or auditorium.
- **Orientation:** Landscape, wide.
- **Crop requirements:** Must read as "big room, big group" at a glance.
- **Participants:** 20+ delegates.
- **Wardrobe:** Conference attire (lanyards, business wear).
- **Lighting:** Venue/stage lighting — may need a second, brighter safety shot for legibility.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Capture varied seating/standing participation.
- **Permissions required:** Event organiser and venue consent; any visible sponsor/venue branding must be cleared or excluded.

### 11. Male-dominated workplace

- **Setting:** Trade office, depot, logistics floor, or standard office with a male-majority team.
- **Orientation:** Landscape.
- **Crop requirements:** N/A beyond standard.
- **Participants:** 4–6 men, varied ages.
- **Wardrobe:** Workwear or business attire as appropriate to the setting.
- **Lighting:** Natural/site-appropriate.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Priority gap.
- **Permissions required:** Individual and site consent.

### 12. Industrial or high-vis environment
- **Status:** Partially covered — `Blog6.jpg` and `Workplace Wellness Lunch & Learn Session at a warehouse in Brisbane.webp` (indoor) plus the outdoor high-vis park group are genuinely valuable, but resolution is modest (892×560 to 2048×2048) and none is a large-format hero-grade capture.
- **Business purpose:** A named blueprint priority ("industrial/high-vis, conferences, distributed teams" — blueprint §33).
- **Page placement:** Movement, Workplace Wellbeing, Programs.
- **Recommended subject:** A standing or seated (e.g. lunchroom) stretch session on an active industrial site.
- **Setting:** Warehouse, depot, construction site amenity area, or manufacturing floor.
- **Orientation:** Landscape, high resolution for hero use.
- **Crop requirements:** Keep high-vis PPE clearly visible.
- **Participants:** 4–8, mixed gender.
- **Wardrobe:** High-vis PPE, hard hats where site-appropriate.
- **Lighting:** Site-appropriate; may require supplementary lighting for a usable hero shot.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Confirm WHS/site safety sign-off for any photo shoot on an active industrial site.
- **Permissions required:** Site/employer consent, WHS approval, individual consent, and (if any site branding is visible) client permission.

### 13. Remote participation
- **Status:** Partially covered — two video-call grid images (`Online Lunch and Learn.webp`, `online-wellbeing.webp`) work well conceptually, but both are UI-chrome-heavy call screenshots rather than photography, and individual participant consent to appear in marketing is undocumented.
- **Business purpose:** Supports "support a distributed workforce" and Wellbeing Studio's remote-access claim.
- **Page placement:** Wellbeing Studio, Workplace Wellbeing, Programs.
- **Recommended subject:** A single real person on a video call from a home office, filmed as photography (not a screenshot) — camera behind/beside the laptop, showing the person and screen together.
- **Setting:** Genuine home office.
- **Orientation:** Landscape or square.
- **Crop requirements:** Include both the person and enough of the screen to read as "joining remotely" without needing UI chrome.
- **Participants:** 1 per shot, several variations.
- **Wardrobe:** Casual/home-office appropriate.
- **Lighting:** Natural home lighting.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Vary home settings/backgrounds to avoid a single "aspirational" home archetype.
- **Permissions required:** Individual consent, explicit marketing-use release (screenshots of real calls carry extra sensitivity).

### 17. Facilitator portraits
- **Status:** Partially covered — five named facilitators have usable portraits (Cadia, Grace, Val, Mickey, TIDA), but consent for marketing use, active status and credentials are undocumented for all of them, and one (Sara) is unusable at native resolution (213×320).
- **Business purpose:** Substantiates "national facilitator network" without overstating capacity.
- **Page placement:** About facilitator-network section.
- **Recommended subject:** Consistent-style headshots (same background, framing, lighting) for every currently active, credentialed facilitator who has signed an image-use release — a proper register shoot, not a mixed set of found images.
- **Setting:** Consistent studio or on-location backdrop across all subjects.
- **Orientation:** Portrait, with a square crop derivative for grid layouts.
- **Crop requirements:** Head-and-shoulders, consistent framing across the set.
- **Participants:** Every active facilitator.
- **Wardrobe:** CYA-branded or neutral professional attire, consistent across the set.
- **Lighting:** Consistent, flattering, repeatable setup.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** Use this shoot to intentionally broaden age/ethnicity/gender representation in the facilitator set.
- **Permissions required:** Signed image-use release per facilitator, tied to the facilitator register (credentials, insurance, active status) called for in the Strategic Source of Truth.

### 20. Wide hero imagery
- **Status:** Partially covered — several images exceed 5000px wide, but almost all are portrait-orientation studio shots (yoga poses) rather than landscape workplace scenes; genuinely wide (2:1 or wider) *workplace* photography is thin. `Workplace Wellness Session.jpg` (6000×3140) is the standout exception.
- **Business purpose:** Every page hero in this build uses a 4:3 crop; a true wide/cinematic hero would need purpose-shot landscape source material with generous negative space for copy.
- **Page placement:** Any page hero, if the component is later extended to support a wider ratio.
- **Recommended subject:** Real workplace group scenes, shot deliberately with 30–40% negative space on one side for headline copy.
- **Setting:** Varies by page (office, warehouse, conference room).
- **Orientation:** Landscape, minimum 2:1.
- **Crop requirements:** Must survive being cropped down to 4:3 and 1:1 as well, so keep the subject within the centre 60% of the frame.
- **Participants:** Varies.
- **Wardrobe:** Varies.
- **Lighting:** Varies.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** N/A beyond standard.
- **Permissions required:** Standard site/individual consent.

### 21. Mobile-friendly portrait crops
- **Status:** Gap — the library is almost entirely sourced/cropped for desktop landscape or square use; nothing was shot with a narrow vertical (4:5 or 9:16) mobile-first frame in mind, and this build's `Hero`/`EditorialFeature` components do not yet implement a separate mobile-portrait art-directed source (see mapping doc, Known limitations).
- **Business purpose:** Most first visits to this site will be on a phone (build prompt, Mobile Experience).
- **Page placement:** Any hero, if/when responsive art-direction is implemented.
- **Recommended subject:** Re-shoot key hero concepts (Home, Workplace Wellbeing, Movement) with a vertical safe-crop in mind — subject positioned so a 4:5 crop still works.
- **Setting:** As per each page's hero subject.
- **Orientation:** Capture wide, but compose so a vertical crop remains meaningful (subject not spread edge-to-edge).
- **Crop requirements:** Provide both a landscape and a pre-approved vertical derivative.
- **Participants:** As per each hero.
- **Wardrobe:** As per each hero.
- **Lighting:** As per each hero.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** N/A beyond standard.
- **Permissions required:** Standard.

### 22. Case-study evidence
- **Status:** Gap — no image in the library is tied to a named, permissioned client engagement with recorded consent. `work wellness sessions.webp` (WalkMe signage visible) is the closest match and is explicitly held pending permission.
- **Business purpose:** Blocks all four Proof/case-study placeholders from becoming real, published stories.
- **Page placement:** Proof and case-study cards/template.
- **Recommended subject:** Delivery-in-progress photography at a confirmed, consented client site — ideally captured alongside the quantitative evidence (attendance, feedback) the case-study template requires, so imagery and data are approved together.
- **Setting:** Real client site.
- **Orientation:** Landscape and square derivatives.
- **Crop requirements:** Avoid cropping out anything that establishes credible scale (room size, group size).
- **Participants:** Client team, CYA facilitator.
- **Wardrobe:** As worn.
- **Lighting:** As available on site.
- **Accessibility/inclusion:** N/A beyond standard.
- **Permissions required:** Full client sign-off (logo, quotation, photography, dates, facts) per the case-study governance model already defined in `content/proof.ts` — this is a business-process gap, not just a photography gap.

## Priority order for the next shoot

If the shoot must be phased, this order clears the highest-visibility gaps first:

1. **Conference activation** (§8) — the Conferences hero currently borrows an unrelated image; this is the single weakest placement in the site relative to its page's job.
2. **Facilitator portrait register** (§17) — currently blocks any named facilitator content from ever publishing.
3. **Male-dominated / industrial workplace** (§11, §12) — closes the largest representation gap in the current library.
4. **Multi-generational team** (§10) and **breathwork** (§6) — both are named, specific, and currently unrepresented.
5. **Case-study evidence** (§22) — dependent on the client-permission workstream closing, not purely a photography task; sequence alongside it rather than waiting for it.
6. **Wide hero + mobile portrait crops** (§20, §21) — a production/technical upgrade once a Hero art-direction component exists, not urgent for the prototype stage.
