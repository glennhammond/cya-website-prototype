# CYA Website — Phase 11.4 Workplace Pilates Publication Decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026  
**Status:** PUBLISH / INDEX-QUALIFIED  
**Canonical:** `/workplace-pilates`

## Decision

Workplace Pilates has sufficient current CYA product evidence to publish as a substantive canonical service page.

This decision supersedes earlier Phase 11.4 language that treated `/workplace-pilates` as a `noindex` production draft or required separate image-permission clearance before publication.

## Evidence threshold crossed

Current CYA public and owned material supports the following real service characteristics:

- mat-based Pilates;
- one-off Pilates classes;
- recurring Pilates sessions / short series;
- mixed Yoga + Pilates scheduling;
- onsite and online movement delivery;
- hybrid / multi-location movement delivery where the participant and technology setup is suitable;
- flexible workplace session lengths and practical workplace adaptation;
- Pilates capability within CYA's facilitator network;
- current public association of a named Sydney facilitator with Yoga, Pilates and Meditation capability.

The service therefore does not depend on a Pilates case study or a publicly displayed practitioner certificate string merely to exist as a search-acquisition page.

## Publication guardrails

### Practitioner credentials

The page does **not** publish an unverified qualification string for an individual practitioner.

For an actual booking, CYA confirms:

- the practitioner;
- relevant Pilates qualification;
- location;
- availability;
- suitability for the workplace brief.

Individual credential details may be added publicly later when the corresponding records have been verified.

### Claims

The page deliberately avoids unsupported promises about:

- injury prevention;
- posture correction;
- pain relief;
- rehabilitation;
- guaranteed strength, stress or health outcomes.

The service is described through format, delivery, workplace fit and practical setup rather than medical/performance claims.

### Photography

All governed CYA website images are publication-approved under the superseding 26 August 2026 image decision.

The current curated set does not contain a distinctly labelled Pilates-specific photograph. Representative approved CYA movement photography may therefore support the page without claiming that the pictured movement is Pilates. A future Pilates-specific shoot remains a visual-quality enhancement, not a publication-permission requirement.

## Search / IA implementation

`/workplace-pilates` is now:

- indexable;
- self-canonical;
- included in `sitemap.xml`;
- included in the Services navigation;
- linked directly from Home;
- linked directly from the Movement hub;
- rendered with Service + BreadcrumbList structured data;
- tested by the Phase 11.4 source/search harness;
- tested by the rendered production HTTP smoke suite.

## Authority boundary

This decision does not change Home's role as CYA's broad `corporate yoga` authority page or Movement's role as the service-selection hub.

Workplace Pilates is a specialist service acquisition page analogous to Workplace Yoga, but it is not being used to manufacture synonym/page sprawl or to force ranking transfer from another URL.
