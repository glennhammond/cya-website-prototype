## 1. Purpose

This register removes the need for Glenn and Deb to reconcile three complete websites manually. It records the material copy decisions between:

1. **Site 1** — the preferred light-led redesign informed by the search/SEO work;
2. **Site 2** — the earlier image-led prototype containing useful copy and service framing; and
3. **the consolidated candidate** — the single review website on the current draft branch.

The consolidated candidate is now the review surface. Sites 1 and 2 remain evidence for the decisions below, not competing launch candidates.

## 5. Image-review method

Each principal page in the Vercel review deployment contains one numbered, review-only image-direction panel. The contact page intentionally has none.

Glenn and Deb only need to decide for each numbered position:

1. whether the image job is correct;
2. whether the proposed subject represents CYA truthfully;
3. whether an existing approved CYA image meets that direction; and
4. whether the crop, permissions and context are suitable.

The review panels are excluded from production. No candidate image should be inserted merely to fill space.
