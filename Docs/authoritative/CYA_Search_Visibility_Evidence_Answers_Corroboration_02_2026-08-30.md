## 2. Governing psychosocial decision

The earlier CYA psychosocial-hazards and workplace-wellbeing discussion is now an explicit implementation constraint, not a side note.

### CYA may say

- workplace wellbeing activities can complement a broader organisational approach;
- CYA can support practical access, participation, communications, delivery and privacy-aware aggregate review;
- an internal sponsor and a clearly bounded Wellbeing Champion can help activate a CYA program;
- work design, working conditions and organisational systems matter alongside wellbeing activities; and
- organisations should use appropriate WHS, clinical, EAP or legal pathways where the need falls outside CYA's scope.

### CYA must not say or imply

- a Yoga, Pilates, mindfulness, workshop or Champion initiative identifies or controls a psychosocial hazard;
- CYA satisfies psychosocial-risk or legal-compliance duties;
- a Wellbeing Champion owns colleague wellbeing, complaints, disclosures, HR, WHS or mental-health care;
- participation data reveals an individual's wellbeing, motivation or health status; or
- CYA delivery causes health, productivity, psychosocial-control or compliance outcomes without suitable evidence.

This boundary follows current Australian and Queensland primary guidance. Safe Work Australia says a PCBU must eliminate psychosocial risks or, where that is not reasonably practicable, minimise them so far as is reasonably practicable. Queensland's Code requires a risk-management process covering identification, assessment, control and review, with worker consultation. Workplace Health and Safety Queensland separately states that workplace wellbeing interventions should complement safety systems and duties, consider work design, and involve workers.

Primary authorities checked:

- [Safe Work Australia — Psychosocial hazards](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/mental-health/psychosocial-hazards)
- [Workplace Health and Safety Queensland — Managing the risk of psychosocial hazards at work Code of Practice 2022](https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/laws-and-compliance/codes-of-practice/managing-the-risk-of-psychosocial-hazards-at-work-code-of-practice-2022)
- [Workplace Health and Safety Queensland — Work health and wellbeing toolkit](https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/safety-and-prevention/health-and-wellbeing/managing-chronic-disease-at-work/work-health-and-wellbeing-toolkit)
- [Workplace Health and Safety Queensland — Benefits of health and wellbeing for work](https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/safety-and-prevention/health-and-wellbeing/why-take-care-of-health-and-wellbeing-at-work/benefits-of-health-and-wellbeing-for-work)
- [World Health Organization — Mental health at work](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-at-work)

The WHO source reinforces the same distinction: prevention of work-related mental-health conditions requires organisational interventions that address working conditions and environments. Individual wellbeing activity is not a substitute for that work.

## 5. Wellbeing Champion evidence route

`config/wellbeing-champion-evidence-route.json` governs the pilot before any public authority page is created.

### Dual objectives remain distinct

| Objective | Owner | Test |
|---|---|---|
| CYA program participation | Corporate Yoga Australia | Whether supported internal activation improves awareness, access, communications and sustained participation in a real CYA program |
| Enablement-product validation | Emerging eLearning Studio | Whether a separately priced, partner-led enablement product solves a real buyer and learner problem |

The eLearning Studio objective cannot borrow a positive CYA delivery result as product validation. CYA value cannot be obscured by technology production.

### Preferred pilot retained

- 2–4 week readiness and baseline period;
- 12 delivery weeks;
- one site, unit or defined cohort;
- one Champion plus a backup contact;
- named senior sponsor and CYA partner lead;
- four CYA check-ins; and
- Week 12 evidence review.

### Paid-pilot entry gates

The offer cannot be presented as a validated product until there is a named buyer, real sponsor and cohort, protected paid time, usable baseline or comparison data, written data/privacy/IP terms, and a locked evaluation plan.

The Champion is an activation contact. The configuration explicitly excludes ownership of employee wellbeing, psychosocial hazards, complaints, bullying or harassment matters, HR, WHS, EAP, counselling, mental-health care and statutory duties.

### Evidence and future authority

Allowed evidence includes invitation reach where reliably available, aggregate registration and attendance, repeat participation, delivery reliability, voluntary feedback, Champion/sponsor experience and enablement-product friction.

The evidence cannot be used to infer individual wellbeing, motivation, health status, causal health or productivity improvement, psychosocial-control effectiveness or legal compliance.

A standalone Wellbeing Champion page is **not authorised before real pilot evidence**. Future assets remain permission, privacy, context and limitations gated.
