# CYA — Phase 11.5 Authority & Backlink Growth Programme v1.0

**Status:** strategic operating model ready; execution begins after migration safety gate  
**Market:** Australia  
**Evidence date:** 26 August 2026

## Objective

Build CYA's external authority through real workplace-wellbeing, business, event, expert and client relationships — not through bulk link acquisition.

The desired outcome is stronger topical/entity authority for CYA's genuine commercial offer: Corporate / Workplace Yoga, Workplace Wellbeing Programs, Workplace Pilates, Mindfulness & Meditation, Workshops & Expert Experiences, and online/hybrid wellbeing continuity.

Links are one evidence signal within that authority system. Domain Rating is not the goal.

## What the Ahrefs evidence says

### Headline link count overstates the useful picture

Ahrefs' current SERP data shows Home with roughly 314 referring domains. Filtered live/non-spam page-level analysis shows the meaningful equity is concentrated much more narrowly:

- `https://www.corporateyoga.com.au/` — 28 live non-spam referring domains in the page-by-backlinks view;
- 23 dofollow links in that filtered page view;
- Home UR approximately 6;
- additional equity is split across non-www, HTTP and historical URL variants.

The migration must consolidate these variants cleanly before new link acquisition becomes a priority.

### Strong existing links are relationship/evidence links

| Source | DR | Link context | Strategic meaning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `australia.com` / Business Events Australia | 81 | Tourism Australia business-events itinerary mentioning a private Yoga session | national business-events authority; high relevance |
| `brisbane.qld.au` / Brisbane Business Hub | 74 | Lord Mayor's Women in Business Grant recipient page | founder/business credibility; civic authority |
| `besydney.com.au` | 51 | wellness activities in Sydney | business events / destination authority |
| `alsco.com.au` | 47 | workplace activities article | workplace-context editorial citation |

These are much more strategically valuable than automated directories, scraper sites or raw link counts.

### Competitor SERPs do not show a huge backlink barrier

For `corporate wellness programs` (AU 500/month, KD 0), current page-one results include Australian sites with modest domain/page authority and often very few page-level referring domains.

Observed examples include Workplace Wellness Australia (DR ~7; 11 page referring domains), Employee Wellness Australia (DR ~10; 17), The Wellness Workshop (DR ~11; around 1), and other page-one Australian results with zero recorded page-level referring domains.

This supports a strategy led by search-intent fit, page quality, topical relevance and entity credibility rather than a high-volume backlink campaign.

For `workplace yoga`, CYA already competes on page one. The SERP includes both a DR 0 domain with hundreds of referring domains and a DR 10 competitor with almost no page-level backlinks. Raw link quantity is therefore a poor optimisation target.

## Authority strategy

### Lane 0 — Preserve and consolidate existing authority

**Priority: P0 / migration**

Before outreach:

1. preserve genuine existing backlinks through one-hop redirects;
2. canonicalise HTTP → HTTPS;
3. canonicalise non-www/www consistently to the selected production host;
4. retire broken historical paths without soft-redirecting irrelevant spam;
5. update quality external links that still point to obsolete paths where relationship access makes that easy;
6. monitor lost referring domains after launch.

The cheapest authority gain is not losing what CYA already earned.

### Lane 1 — Client and workplace relationship authority

**Priority: highest ongoing**

Potential mechanisms include client wellbeing/people-and-culture pages where public linking is appropriate, supplier/partner pages, case-study co-publication after permission, event/conference partner listings, employee wellbeing resource pages, partner interviews and genuine source-attributed testimonials.

Target sectors should follow actual CYA relationships, not invented vertical pages.

**Quality test:** would this reference make sense if Google did not exist?

### Lane 2 — Business events, venues and property/workplace ecosystems

Business Events Australia and BESydney are already two of CYA's best links; competitor profiles also show event/venue/property links.

Priority ecosystems include convention bureaux, conference venues, coworking providers, commercial property/workplace operators, event-production partners, corporate retreat/event planners and destination/event wellbeing guides.

Useful collaborative assets may include a conference wellbeing planning guide, movement/reset formats by agenda length, speaker/expert experience menus and venue-space requirements for Yoga/Pilates/mindfulness activations.

Do not create thin venue/location doorway pages.

### Lane 3 — Expert / professional authority network

CYA's Expert Experiences model is a natural authority engine.

For each genuine expert/session:

- maintain a credible CYA expert profile;
- link to the expert's professional site/profile where useful;
- seek natural editorial attribution, not reciprocal-link quotas;
- produce a substantive expert conversation/podcast page with notes/transcript/resources;
- give experts a useful canonical page they may naturally reference;
- connect relevant Insights to the expert/session and commercial service.

Potential authority categories include psychology, psychosocial safety, physiotherapy, nutrition/dietetics, menopause, sleep, financial wellbeing, leadership and workplace culture — only where CYA has a real expert/product relationship.

### Lane 4 — Organisations, associations and professional ecosystems

Competitor profiles show useful links from EAP/workplace/business organisations rather than only wellness sites.

Pursue legitimate editorial/resource inclusion with HR/people-and-culture associations, EAP/workplace wellbeing ecosystems, business chambers, industry associations where CYA genuinely participates, workplace safety/health communities, event associations and credible Australian business publications.

Do not pay for meaningless association-directory links simply for DR.

### Lane 5 — Link-worthy CYA authority assets

Priority asset candidates:

1. **Australian Workplace Wellbeing Calendar** — strengthen the canonical web version and make annual updates referenceable.
2. **How to introduce Yoga at work** — protect and upgrade the existing authority article with current, evidence-safe implementation guidance.
3. **Workplace wellbeing program planning guide** — procurement-friendly framework for HR/People teams.
4. **Conference & event wellbeing planning guide** — strongly aligned with existing Business Events authority.
5. **Practical campaign/reset resources** — including R U OK? Day where CYA has substantive campaign content.
6. **Expert insight series** — substantive interview/resource pages, not thin show notes.
7. **Evidence/reporting explainer** — what useful workplace wellbeing reporting can and cannot show.

Create these because they are genuinely useful; earning links is a secondary outcome. Avoid pseudo-research, invented statistics and unsupported outcome claims.

### Lane 6 — Selective digital PR

Use PR only when there is a genuine story: credible Australian workplace wellbeing data/insight, a meaningful campaign/program finding, founder/business milestone, expert panel/national initiative, useful annual planning data or conference/event collaboration.

Do not manufacture press releases solely for backlinks.

## Competitor-link patterns worth learning from

Current competitor profiles include useful patterns such as podcast/editorial exposure (`transistor.fm`), workplace/EAP association (`eapaa.org.au`), business publications (`thetimes.com.au`, `businessacumen.biz`), property/workplace ecosystems (`commercialpropertyguide.com.au`, `workit.com.au`), event/venue relationships (`leapevents.com`, venue sites), care/employer/client relationships (`mercycare.com.au`, `tricare.com.au`) and expert/professional ecosystems (`consultclarity.org`).

These are pattern evidence, not a mechanical outreach list. Every prospect needs a real editorial or relationship reason to reference CYA.

## What not to pursue

Reject bulk directory submissions, paid dofollow packages, PBNs, automated guest-post networks, unrelated high-DR domains, sitewide footer links, exact-match anchor campaigns, reciprocal-link quotas, scraper/SEO-metric sites, thin city pages, fake awards/research and links requiring unsupported health or ROI claims.

The presence of scraper/SEO domains in CYA and competitor profiles is evidence to filter harder, not imitate them.

## 90-day execution programme

### Days 0–14 — protect

- complete production migration and canonical-host consolidation;
- monitor lost/new referring domains;
- identify top existing quality links landing on old URLs or non-canonical host variants;
- confirm the four strongest existing authority relationships remain live;
- capture an initial live non-spam referring-domain baseline after recrawl;
- do no broad outreach while migration behaviour is still unclear.

### Days 15–30 — reclaim and package

- update high-quality external links where they unnecessarily pass through obsolete URLs;
- prepare a simple partner/expert linking kit: canonical URL, approved description and image/logo where appropriate;
- select the first 2–3 existing CYA assets to strengthen for referenceability;
- identify 10 warm relationship opportunities from real clients, experts, venues and business-event partners.

### Days 31–60 — relationship acquisition

Aim for quality opportunities rather than a link quota:

- 3–5 client/partner/editorial references;
- 2–3 expert authority connections;
- 2 event/property/business ecosystem opportunities;
- strengthen at least one genuinely link-worthy CYA resource;
- pitch one useful expert/editorial contribution where a clear audience fit exists.

These are planning ranges, not quotas requiring low-quality substitutes.

### Days 61–90 — compound what works

- review which relationship lanes produced real traffic, authority or commercial visibility;
- compare new referring domains with competitor patterns;
- expand only productive lanes;
- consider a small digital-PR push around a genuine CYA asset/story;
- connect earned authority to priority commercial destinations through sensible internal linking;
- evaluate whether any topic/location now has enough product + evidence + demand to justify further search architecture.

## Opportunity scoring

Score each candidate 0–3 on relevance, authority/trust, relationship strength, editorial fit, commercial adjacency, durability and effort (reverse score: low effort = 3). Prioritise high-total opportunities.

Domain Rating is contextual metadata, not a scoring category by itself.

## Measurement

Track monthly:

- new/lost **quality** referring domains;
- quality links to priority commercial/service pages;
- links still passing through obsolete hosts/paths;
- referral sessions and meaningful enquiries;
- branded/non-branded visibility;
- Rank Tracker share of voice once configured;
- growth of program/service query clusters;
- AI/search citations where useful later;
- relationship lane that produced each earned reference.

Do not report `total backlinks acquired` as the primary success metric.

## First recommended targets

The first authority effort after migration should be relationship recovery and expansion, in this order:

1. preserve/refresh existing Business Events Australia / Tourism Australia relevance;
2. preserve the Brisbane Business Hub founder/business authority link;
3. preserve/expand BESydney/business-events relationships;
4. identify additional conference/venue/property partners through CYA's real delivery history;
5. package expert-session pages so participating experts can naturally reference them;
6. use approved client relationships for substantive proof/collaboration only after permission;
7. strengthen the annual Wellbeing Calendar and Yoga-at-Work authority resources.

---

**Decision principle:** earn references by being useful, credible and genuinely connected to Australian workplaces. Search authority should be a consequence of CYA's real business authority, not parallel link-building theatre.
