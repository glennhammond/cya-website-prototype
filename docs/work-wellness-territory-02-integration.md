# Work Wellness Territory 02 — Semantic Integration

Status: implementation candidate
Date: 2026-09-01

## Purpose

Extend the governed Work Wellness concept from a single authority page into a coherent CYA entity and service system without replacing established workplace wellbeing buyer/search language.

## Semantic model

Corporate Yoga Australia is the entity.

Workplace wellbeing and employee wellbeing are established market/search categories.

Work Wellness is CYA's distinctive conceptual lens: useful wellbeing that becomes part of working life rather than sitting outside it.

Work Wellness into Your Workday is the external brand expression.

Wellbeing Studio is a practical delivery expression of the concept, not a renamed Work Wellness product.

## Territory 02 integrations

- Organization schema now includes the brand slogan and explicit `knowsAbout` relationships for workplace wellbeing, employee wellbeing, Work Wellness and principal service categories.
- The canonical Work Wellness authority page publishes a `DefinedTerm` entity alongside Article schema.
- About CYA now explains how the founder story and belief that wellbeing belongs in the workday connect to the Work Wellness lens.
- Online Wellbeing now demonstrates Work Wellness in practice and explicitly positions Wellbeing Studio as continuity within the same CYA workplace wellbeing relationship.
- Territory 01 homepage and Insights links remain the primary discovery routes.

## Guardrails retained

1. Do not claim CYA invented or exclusively owns the phrase Work Wellness.
2. Do not replace workplace wellbeing in commercial page titles, service naming or buyer-facing search architecture.
3. Do not add Work Wellness to primary navigation at this stage.
4. Do not rename Wellbeing Studio.
5. Do not present yoga, mindfulness, Pilates or Wellbeing Studio as controls for psychosocial hazards.
6. Use Work Wellness only where it explains the CYA worldview or connects otherwise separate delivery modes.
7. Prefer one strong contextual use over repeated exact-match phrasing.

## Measurement baseline for the 90-day validation period

Track at minimum:

- work wellness
- work wellness australia
- work wellness programs
- work wellness activities
- workplace wellbeing
- workplace wellbeing programs
- employee wellbeing
- wellbeing at work
- workplace wellness

Observe:

- Google Search Console impressions, clicks and ranking URLs;
- whether `/blog/what-is-work-wellness` begins receiving impressions for adjacent category queries rather than only exact-match terms;
- external links and unlinked mentions combining Corporate Yoga Australia with Work Wellness or workplace wellbeing;
- AI citations and mentions for CYA across workplace wellbeing and Work Wellness prompts;
- whether prospective clients repeat the phrase without being prompted;
- assisted journeys from the authority page into workplace wellbeing programmes, Online Wellbeing and contact.

## Escalation rule

Remain at Level B unless evidence develops across more than one channel. Level C category-building should require sustained search behaviour plus independent third-party adoption or citation plus measurable association between Corporate Yoga Australia and the term.
